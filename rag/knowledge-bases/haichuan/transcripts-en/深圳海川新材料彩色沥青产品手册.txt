2 Colored Asphalt Products

Functions of colored asphalt concrete pavement: used mainly on urban roads such as parks, residential areas, scenic areas, sightseeing roads, plazas and amusement parks, to beautify the environment and improve the road environment, raising the city's image. Used on bus-only lanes, intersections, etc., it guides traffic, eases driver fatigue, directs traffic flow, and increases traffic safety. Colored asphalt concrete pavement is bright in color with high reflectivity, reducing pavement heat absorption and the urban heat-island effect. Colored permeable asphalt concrete pavement drains water, reduces noise, eliminates ponding, and supports sponge-city construction.

Advantages of colored asphalt concrete pavement:
- Guides traffic flow, increases traffic safety
- Drains water and reduces noise, supports sponge-city construction
- Eases fatigue, increases driving safety
- Beautifies the environment, raises city taste
- Increases reflectivity, lowers pavement temperature

Classification of colored pavement paving (by surface material):
- Colored asphalt concrete pavement: flexible pavement, little affected by temperature change, long-lasting color, moderate price, environmentally friendly
- Colored polymer pavement: bright colors, many color options, high friction coefficient, shorter braking distance, fast curing
- Colored cement concrete pavement: surface wears easily, hard to repair once damaged, low price
- Colored block pavement: poor bonding between blocks and the layer below, blocks prone to falling out, lower price, rich colors
- Colored elastic rubber pavement: limited to playgrounds/tracks, complex construction, expensive, not environmentally friendly

RoadPower Color Asphalt Binder: colored asphalt binder generally refers to a light-colored binder — a new paving material with properties similar to conventional asphalt, often called colored asphalt. RoadPower colored asphalt binder is developed using advanced foreign technology, per the national standard for asphalt mixtures. With the addition of a polymer modifier, its plasticity is greatly improved and its ductility is noticeably better than ordinary asphalt.

RoadPower Color Asphalt Colorant: drawing on Haichuan's strong expertise in architectural color design and representation methods, we provide full-spectrum positioning, packaging, and design of colorants for colored asphalt concrete pavement, so our products meet the needs of a wide range of customers in every respect.

RoadPower colored asphalt binder transport methods:
1. Tanker transport (20-30 t/tanker, can connect directly to the batching plant) (recommended — convenient and fast)
2. Drum transport (190 kg/drum, requires drum-unloading equipment)

Color stability: color score >=80

Colored pavement paving design scheme:
1. By application area: pedestrian/bike lanes, municipal greenways, bus-only lanes, light-vehicle lanes, scenic roads, expressway service areas, cycling lanes, leisure plazas, industrial parks
2. Colored layer thickness: typically 2-4 cm as needed
3. Typical colored asphalt concrete pavement structure: colored asphalt concrete surface layer / waterproof layer / middle-and-lower layer / base layer
4. Colored-layer color: a wide range of colors available as needed (iron oxide yellow, iron oxide green, iron oxide red, iron oxide black, iron oxide brown, iron oxide blue, iron oxide orange, chromium oxide green)

Mix design steps: 1) select the asphalt and mineral aggregate; 2) determine the mineral aggregate ratio; 3) determine the asphalt content; 4) after gradation design, replace part of the mineral filler with colorant equal to 2.5% of total mixture mass, then test to determine the optimum oil-stone ratio.

Construction process:
Construction requirements: colored asphalt mixture pavement should not be constructed when the air temperature is below 10C. Construction between 0-10C requires effective quality-assurance measures; construction below 0C or in strong wind in winter should not proceed, and colored asphalt concrete should not be laid in rain.

Construction temperatures:
- Colored asphalt binder heating temperature: 140-150C
- Sand/stone aggregate heating temperature: 160-180C
- Mineral filler and colorant: not heated
- Air temperature >=10C: mixture discharge temperature 140-160C, temperature at the site >=140C
- Air temperature 0-10C: mixture discharge temperature 150-170C, temperature at the site >=150C
Note: because colorant heat tolerance varies, the actual construction temperature should be adjusted to suit the specific colorant used.

Compaction requirements (roller type / roller weight / initial rolling speed m/min / medium rolling speed m/min):
- Steel (smooth-wheel) roller: 6-8 t double-wheel: 25-35 / 40-55
- Vibratory roller: 6-7 t, static rolling: 25-35 (static) / 30-35 (static)
- Approximate passes: 2 / 2-4 (until essentially no wheel marks)

3 Colored Anti-Skid Polymer Pavement

Introduction: colored anti-skid polymer pavement has been widely used in European countries but is still a relatively new concept in China. As traffic volume grows unprecedentedly, congestion is beginning to appear, especially in city centers. In Europe, among many traffic management solutions, laying colored anti-skid polymer pavement is a primary approach, widely used for traffic channelization and deceleration cues. At accident-prone locations such as ramps, curves, tunnel entrances/exits, intersections, crosswalks, roundabouts, bus-only lanes, bus stops, and expressway toll plazas, adding a colored anti-skid pavement layer significantly improves wet-skid and side-slip resistance, providing a strong visual warning plus anti-skid function.

Advantages of colored anti-skid polymer pavement:
1. Increases friction, shortens braking distance
2. Wear- and weather-resistant, extends pavement life
3. Distinctive structure, reduces driving noise
4. Beautifies the environment, raises city taste
5. Guides traffic flow, increases traffic safety
6. Increases reflectivity, lowers pavement temperature

Product overview: RoadPower colored anti-skid polymer pavement consists of resin component A and B, wear-resistant aggregate, and a UV-resistant wear-resistant top coat. Components A and B are mixed at a set ratio and spread, then colored anti-skid aggregate is spread evenly; once cured, excess aggregate is swept off and a UV-resistant wear-resistant top coat is applied (rolled or sprayed) to form a colored anti-skid surface layer with substantial texture depth. Structure from top to bottom: ceramic granules, anti-skid top coat, anti-skid middle coat, anti-skid base coat, cement substrate.

Colored anti-skid polymer pavement adhesive: a new polymer material independently developed by Haichuan. During construction, component B (hardener) is added to component A, triggering a fast network cross-linking reaction that ultimately forms a high-strength, highly adherent film, achieving durability under the special conditions of road use.

Ceramic granules: common colors are red, green, yellow, and blue surface layers — rich, long-lasting colors; colored anti-skid pavement's skid resistance value stays stably above 70. The surface color of the pavement system is determined by the chosen aggregate color.

Colored anti-skid polymer pavement adhesive technical indicators (test item / non-motor-vehicle lane use / motor-vehicle lane use):
- Base material condition in container: no clumping, no skinning, easy to stir evenly
- Gel time, min: >=10 / >=10
- Tensile strength (25C), MPa: >=6 / >=7
- Elongation at break, %: >=20 / >=35

Ceramic granule technical indicators: Mohs hardness >=6; aggregate particle size <=4mm

Colored anti-skid polymer pavement system performance indicators: texture depth, mm: 1.6-2.0; skid-resistance pendulum value (BPN): 75-85

Colored anti-skid polymer pavement thickness design (traffic class / volume / pavement thickness):
- Light: <=4,500 PCU/day/lane: 1-2mm
- Medium: 4,500-6,450 PCU/day/lane: 2-4mm
- Heavy: >=6,450 PCU/day/lane: 2-5mm

Colored anti-skid polymer pavement construction process: 1. Clean and dry 2. Partition and protect 3. Base coat construction 4. Add hardener to the middle coat 5. Middle coat construction 6. Spread aggregate 7. Collect excess aggregate 8. Top coat construction 9. Open to traffic

4 Colored Asphalt Repair Agent

Colored asphalt has been developing in China for over a decade; as time passes, older colored asphalt concrete pavements age and fade, losing their original attractive appearance. Our in-house developed colored asphalt repair agent, made of a specialty polymer resin and aggregate of different specifications, is sprayed or rolled onto old pavement in a single layer to restore the color of the asphalt concrete pavement, making it look fresh again. Its unique chemical structure bonds well with colored asphalt and forms a 3D network structure, giving the repair agent excellent wear and weathering resistance.

Uses: applied to urban roads such as crosswalk pavement, fully paved areas, directional pavement, urban ramps, toll-booth dedicated lanes, landscaping beautification, cycling lanes, and sidewalks — to strengthen traffic-safety guidance and express the city's humanistic spirit.

Mainly used for: 1) pre-maintenance or maintenance of colored asphalt concrete pavement, restoring its original bright color; 2) coloring black asphalt concrete pavement; 3) coloring cement concrete pavement.

Thickness design: 0.5-1mm recommended, adjustable as needed.
Color: adjustable to customer needs; common colors are red, green, yellow, blue, and grey.

Technical analysis:
- Water- and moisture-resistant, extends pavement life
- Good low-temperature resistance, suits different regions
- Easy to construct, no large equipment needed
- Firmly bonded, does not peel or come loose

Colored asphalt repair agent technical indicators (test item / technical indicator):
- Base material condition in container: no clumping, no skinning, easy to stir evenly
- Gel time, min: >=10
- Base material adhesion (cross-hatch method), grade: <=4
- Tack-free drying time, h: <=1
- Low-temperature crack resistance of the coating: no cracking
- Accelerated weathering resistance: no cracking or peeling

Colored asphalt repair agent construction process: 1. Clean and dry 2. Result after cleaning 3. Add hardener to the repair agent and stir 4. Spray or roll on the repair agent 5. Result after repair
