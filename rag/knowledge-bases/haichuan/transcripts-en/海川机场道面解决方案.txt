1 Challenges Facing Airport Pavement

Compared with ordinary highways, airport pavement bears far heavier loads (heavy aircraft wheel-load pressure can exceed 1.0-1.5 MPa), placing significantly higher demands on high-temperature rutting resistance, skid resistance, durability, and impact resistance. In addition, different functional zones — runway, taxiway, apron — emphasize different material properties:
- Runway: high skid resistance, wear resistance, impact resistance, fatigue resistance
- Taxiway: high-temperature rutting resistance is especially important
- Apron: resistance to oil-contamination corrosion and static-load deformation

Airport pavement also faces:
1) Material stability requirements under large diurnal temperature swings and extreme climate conditions;
2) Long-term static loading and exposure to fuel, hydraulic oil, and other chemical media in apron areas;
3) Long-term wear resistance requirements for the skid-resistant surface texture in runway areas;
4) Short maintenance windows, requiring fast-curing, fast-opening-to-traffic material solutions.

2 Haichuan Airport Pavement Material Solutions

1) Anti-rutting solution — Rad spunrie ("Rutting King")
Addresses high-temperature rutting resistance needs on airport pavement, at a dosage of 0.3%-0.7% of the asphalt mixture weight, significantly improving pavement stability under high temperature and heavy load, reducing the incidence of rutting and shoving. See "Shenzhen Haichuan Anti-Rutting Product Manual."

2) High-viscosity modification solution — SINOTPS high-viscosity modifier
Used to produce high-performance SMA mixtures suitable for airport pavement and steel bridge-deck paving, with excellent road performance and shear/fatigue resistance. See "Haichuan High-Viscosity Product Manual."

3) Flame-retardant solution — FRMAX™ flame-retardant modifier
Suitable for hangars, underground facilities, and other areas with special fire-safety requirements. See "Haichuan Flame-Retardant Product Manual."

4) Warm-mix solution — EC series warm-mix additives
Used to shorten construction periods and lower construction energy consumption, suited to time-limited construction windows in airport expansion/renovation projects. See "Shenzhen Haichuan Warm-Mix Product Manual."

5) GFRP fiber-reinforced composite material solution
Applied to airport pavement structural reinforcement, expansion-joint treatment, and slope support, with corrosion resistance, high strength, and light weight. See "Haichuan Fiber-Reinforced Composite Material (GFRP) Product Manual."

6) Regeneration solution — OP series regenerant
Suitable for recycling old asphalt mixture during major repair/renovation of airport pavement, reducing project cost and environmental impact. See "Haichuan Regenerant Product Manual."

3 Recommended Typical Airport Pavement Structure Design

Surface layer: upper layer uses high-viscosity modified asphalt SMA or a dense-graded asphalt mixture with Rad spunrie, balancing skid resistance and rutting resistance;
Middle layer: medium-graded asphalt mixture with Rad spunrie for improved high-temperature stability;
Base layer: cement-stabilized macadam or semi-rigid base depending on subgrade conditions, combined with GFRP material for structural reinforcement where needed.
