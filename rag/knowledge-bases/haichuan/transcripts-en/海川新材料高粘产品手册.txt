Green Road — Haichuan SINOTPS Asphalt Concrete High-Viscosity Modifier: The Best Choice for Porous (Drainage/Noise-Reducing) Asphalt Concrete Pavement

Porous, noise-reducing asphalt concrete adjusts the gradation of coarse and fine aggregate to raise the void ratio to about 20%, so that rainwater falling on the pavement surface is rapidly drained through the large number of voids, avoiding surface ponding and a water film on the pavement, thereby reducing vehicle skidding and spray. Combining excellent drainage, anti-skid, and noise-reduction functions, it is a safe, environmentally friendly new ecological asphalt concrete pavement technology.

1 Introduction to Porous, Noise-Reducing Asphalt Concrete Pavement

1 Functional characteristics

1) Effectively solves pavement ponding: rain drains through the voids within the surface layer rather than forming a film and runoff on the surface, mitigating flood risk.
2) Improves driving safety: in rainy weather, porous noise-reducing pavement effectively reduces ponding, increases tire-to-pavement adhesion, prevents hydroplaning, suppresses spray/fog that affects following vehicles' vision, and reduces glare — significantly improving safety. Statistics show that after this pavement is laid, the traffic accident rate drops by 85%.
3) Reduces environmental noise: compared with ordinary asphalt concrete, porous noise-reducing asphalt concrete pavement can reduce noise by 3-5 dB.

2 Structural characteristics

Mixture comparison (porous asphalt mixture / SMA / ordinary dense-graded asphalt mixture):
- Internal structure: porous mixture void ratio ~20%, coarse aggregate 80%, fine aggregate 10%, filler 5%, asphalt 5%
- SMA: void ratio ~3%, coarse aggregate 70%, fine aggregate 15%, filler 8%, asphalt 7%
- Ordinary dense-graded mixture: void ratio ~5%, coarse aggregate 50%, fine aggregate 40%, filler 5%, asphalt 5%

Compared with conventional dense-graded asphalt concrete, the large-void nature of porous noise-reducing asphalt concrete makes it more vulnerable to sunlight, air, and water erosion, which can reduce mixture durability and cause loss of drainage function. High-quality aggregate and high-viscosity asphalt binder are therefore essential to ensure durability and long-term function.

3 Typical pavement structure design: 4 cm OGFC-13 upper layer, waterproof bonding layer, 6 cm medium-grained asphalt mixture (e.g. AC-20) middle layer, with lower layer and base built to standard design.

2 Product Introduction

SINOTPS is a high-viscosity asphalt modifier independently developed by Haichuan to meet the requirements of China's expressway development. It can be added to base asphalt to produce high-viscosity asphalt, or dry-mixed with aggregate in the mixing drum before the base asphalt is added, to produce high-viscosity asphalt mixture directly. The resulting high-viscosity asphalt mixture shows significantly improved durability, high-temperature stability and anti-aging performance, with good resistance to raveling, water damage, weathering, and flow.

4 Other Applications of SINOTPS High-Viscosity Asphalt

Besides building porous noise-reducing asphalt concrete pavement, SINOTPS has the following uses:

1 Semi-flexible pavement: semi-flexible pavement fills an open-graded, large-void base asphalt concrete with a special cement-based mortar, forming a composite pavement that combines characteristics of asphalt concrete and cement concrete pavements. Semi-flexible pavement has flexibility exceeding cement concrete pavement and rigidity exceeding ordinary asphalt concrete pavement — a new rigid-flexible pavement type. It relies on interlocking: the mutual interlock between aggregate particles plus the grouted cement slurry together develop material strength, improving the pavement's resistance to loading.

2 Stress-absorbing layer: a high-viscosity-asphalt stress-absorbing layer is formed by spraying high-viscosity asphalt then spreading graded crushed stone on top, to absorb stress and provide waterproofing. It is used specifically to address reflective cracking on pavements and bridge decks. Characteristics:
- Good shear strength: reduces relative movement between the asphalt concrete surface layer and the old cement pavement
- Strong deformation resistance and good flexibility: reduces and prevents reflective cracking in the asphalt concrete surface layer
- Good waterproofing: blocks surface water infiltration, eliminating water damage to the pavement
- Enhances bonding between the asphalt concrete surface layer and old cement concrete pavement, eliminating interlayer slippage and reducing tensile stress/strain in the asphalt concrete layer caused by temperature changes

3 Bridge-deck waterproof bonding layer: a high-viscosity asphalt waterproof bonding layer uses high-viscosity modified asphalt as the binder to form a special asphalt-and-crushed-stone structure, with excellent waterproofing and bonding function. It increases bonding between the base layer and the asphalt concrete surface layer, absorbs stress from deformation of the cement concrete base slab, delays reflective cracking, and provides excellent waterproofing, bonding, and interlayer stress-absorption. It also blends well with the asphalt mixture to form an integrated impermeable layer, separating water in the asphalt concrete surface layer from the cement concrete slab and providing mutual protection.

4 High-performance SMA: SINOTPS high-viscosity asphalt is used to prepare high-performance high-viscosity SMA, with excellent road performance and good shear/fatigue resistance, currently used mainly in steel bridge-deck pavement and airport pavement.
