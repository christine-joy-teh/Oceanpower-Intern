Company Introduction

Shenzhen Haichuan New Materials Technology Co., Ltd. (also branded Oceanpower / Opmaterial Haichuan New Materials) is a national-level high-tech enterprise long engaged in the R&D, production, and engineering application of glass fiber reinforced polymer (GFRP) composite materials, and one of the earliest Chinese companies to systematically apply GFRP in rail transit, highway guardrails, and slope support.

1 Product Introduction

GFRP (Glass Fiber Reinforced Polymer) is a new composite material reinforced with glass fiber in a resin matrix, produced by a pultrusion process. It offers excellent corrosion resistance, light weight with high strength, electrical and magnetic non-conductivity, and is easy to cut. Haichuan's GFRP product line includes:

1) GFRP rebar (sand-coated glass fiber rebar): used for pavement reinforcement, metro tunnel segment secondary lining, underground foundations, coastal development, and photovoltaic mounting systems — offering corrosion resistance, durability, light weight, and easy construction. It is an alternative to traditional steel rebar in corrosive environments and in projects with special electromagnetic-shielding requirements.

2) GFRP rock bolts/soil nails (solid, all-thread, hollow, self-drilling): used for tunnel and mine roadway support and slope reinforcement; corrosion-resistant, non-magnetic, lightweight, and easy to cut for convenient transport and installation.

3) Glass fiber anchor cable: used for docks, dams, mine roadway support, concrete road/bridge reinforcement, corrosive environments, hospital NMR/CT facilities, fiber-optic cable reinforcement, telecom towers, and substations.

4) GFRP tie rods (structural tie bars): formwork tie connectors for building/construction projects — can be cut, need no secondary removal, and do not rust once left inside the concrete after construction, with no impact on structural durability.

5) GFRP mesh: a geogrid-type product used for pavement reinforcement and slope protection.

6) GFRP concrete guardrail (new glass-fiber-rebar-reinforced concrete guardrail): used as crash barriers on highways and urban roads. Compared with traditional steel-rebar concrete guardrails, it offers corrosion resistance, does not rust, and has a longer service life; the structural design can also eliminate the traditional climb-arresting ledge.

3 Product Applications — GFRP Concrete Guardrail Engineering Cases and Crash Testing

The new glass-fiber-rebar concrete guardrail has been verified through real-vehicle crash tests with small cars, large buses, and large trucks:

Small-car crash test: all indicators for the new glass-fiber-rebar concrete guardrail met the evaluation standard, demonstrating good safety protection for small vehicles. The new structure eliminates the traditional climb-arresting ledge compared with the conventional structure; small-car crash results showed no adverse impact from removing the ledge, verifying the structural design's analysis of the ledge's role.

Large-bus crash test: all indicators for the new guardrail met the evaluation standard, demonstrating good safety protection for large buses; results from the bus-crash indicators showed removing the climb-arresting ledge had no adverse impact, further verifying the design analysis from a large-bus perspective.

Large-truck crash test: after a truck collision with the new guardrail, a small amount of concrete spalling appeared at the top of the concrete wall, but no structural overturning failure occurred, showing the new guardrail has good resistance to repeated impacts and good crash performance. During the truck collision, the front bumper and the headlight on the impact side detached, and the truck body on the impact side deformed with the front wheel coming loose; however, the truck's glass remained intact, showing the new guardrail also provides good cushioning protection for large-truck occupants.

GFRP concrete guardrail application cases: Yunnan Changbao Expressway, Meizhou Huayang Expressway, Zhuhai Hegang Expressway, Zhongjiang Reconstruction and Expansion, Jiangmen Zhongkai Expressway, Chaozhou Donglianluo Line, and other expressway guardrail projects.
