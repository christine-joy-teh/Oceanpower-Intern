Green Road — Haichuan OP Series Asphalt Concrete Regenerant: The Best Partner for Asphalt Concrete Regeneration

Asphalt concrete regeneration technology mills, recovers, crushes and screens old asphalt pavement, then re-mixes it with a regenerant, new asphalt, and new aggregate at a set ratio so the resulting mixture meets the required road performance and can be relaid as a pavement surface. Asphalt concrete regeneration is an economical, environmentally friendly construction technology.

1 Product Introduction

Haichuan has developed two regenerant products: OP900 and OP1100. The OP series road asphalt regenerant is a brown liquid whose main component is a modified alkyl amine polyamine; it is viscous and flowable at room temperature and is an active additive used specifically in hot-mix asphalt concrete. Adding a set amount of regenerant adjusts the components of the aged asphalt, re-coordinating them and improving the aged asphalt's cementing structure, thereby restoring the asphalt's original properties.

Product features:
- Good compatibility and broad applicability with aged asphalt materials
- Improves the road performance of aged asphalt with a relatively long service life
- Does not destroy the effective components in the asphalt
- Contains no wax, no water
- Easy to store, non-toxic

Complies with the petrochemical industry standard "Hot-Mix Asphalt Regenerant" NB/SH/T 0819-2010.

2 Technical Analysis

Significant economic benefits: statistics show that 13% of China's roads require maintenance each year, producing about 180 million tonnes of waste asphalt concrete pavement material annually — and this figure is growing year on year. Recycling this waste through asphalt concrete regeneration technology saves large amounts of asphalt and aggregate resources, substantially reduces project investment, saves construction funds, and brings major economic benefits.

Good environmental benefits: because of its chemical inertness, asphalt is extremely difficult to degrade naturally and can affect the ecology of landfill sites and surrounding areas, as well as local residents' food and drinking water quality, for a long time. Waste asphalt concrete regeneration technology fundamentally solves this problem and has excellent environmental benefits.

Resource conservation: large-scale application of waste asphalt concrete regeneration technology reduces demand for virgin asphalt, saving petroleum resources, and also reduces the need to quarry large quantities of sand and crushed stone from nature, protecting mountain and landscape resources.

Good performance (test item / AC-13 regenerated asphalt concrete test value / specification value in humid regions):
- Immersed Marshall test residual stability (%): 87 (spec >80)
- Freeze-thaw splitting residual strength ratio (%): 84 (spec >75)
- Rutting test dynamic stability (cycles/mm): 1129 (spec >1000)

Applications: Haichuan's OP series regenerant is suitable for both plant hot in-place recycling and in-place hot recycling of old asphalt pavement. OP-900 is suitable for regenerating heavy-duty (penetration-grade) asphalt, and OP-1100 is suitable for regenerating modified asphalt.
