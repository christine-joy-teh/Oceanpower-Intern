## Sand-coated GFRP rebar / 覆砂 GFRP 玻璃纤维筋

### Q: What is sand-coated GFRP rebar?

Sand-coated GFRP rebar is fiber-reinforced polymer reinforcement made from glass fiber and a resin matrix with a sand-coated surface. The catalogue positions it as corrosion resistant, lightweight, non-conductive, nonmagnetic, and suitable for applications where conventional steel reinforcement may be affected by corrosion or electromagnetic constraints.

### 问：什么是覆砂 GFRP 玻璃纤维筋？

覆砂 GFRP 玻璃纤维筋是由玻璃纤维与树脂基体组成、表面带覆砂层的纤维增强复合材料筋。目录列出的特点包括耐腐蚀、重量轻、不导电和无磁性，适用于钢筋可能受到腐蚀或项目有电磁限制的场景。

### Q: Where can GFRP rebar be used?

Catalogue-listed applications include pavement reinforcement, metro tunnel shield works, underground foundations, coastal development, and photovoltaic support systems. Suitability for a specific project requires engineering confirmation.

### 问：GFRP 玻璃纤维筋可以用于哪些场景？

目录列出的应用包括路面加固、地铁盾构工程、地下基础、沿海开发工程和光伏支架系统。具体项目是否适用仍需工程师确认。

### Q: What is the tensile strength of 18-25 mm sand-coated GFRP rebar?

The catalogue gives a guaranteed tensile-strength reference range of 550-800 MPa for 18-25 mm sand-coated GFRP rebar. The guaranteed shear-strength reference range is 120-150 MPa, and the elasticity modulus is above 40 GPa. Engineering must confirm the exact product grade and design value.

### 问：18-25 毫米覆砂 GFRP 玻璃纤维筋的抗拉强度是多少？

目录给出的保证抗拉强度参考范围为 550-800 MPa，保证抗剪强度参考范围为 120-150 MPa，弹性模量大于 40 GPa。准确的产品等级和设计取值必须由工程师确认。

## BFRP and CFRP / BFRP 与 CFRP

### Q: What are the characteristics of BFRP rebar?

The catalogue describes BFRP rebar as nonmagnetic, electrically insulating, resistant to acid and alkali, temperature resistant, and heat and sound insulating. Catalogue reference values include tensile strength of at least 750 MPa, elongation of at least 1.8%, and alkali strength retention of at least 85%.

### 问：玄武岩纤维筋有什么特点？

目录将 BFRP 玄武岩纤维筋描述为无磁性、电绝缘、耐酸碱、耐温，并具有隔热和隔音特性。目录参考值包括抗拉强度不低于 750 MPa、伸长率不低于 1.8%，以及碱处理后的强度保留率不低于 85%。

### Q: What are the catalogue values for CFRP rebar?

The catalogue lists CFRP rebar diameters of 3-40 mm, density of 1.5-1.6 g/cm3, tensile strength of 1800-2500 MPa, elasticity modulus of 120-165 GPa, and elongation of 1.3-1.5%. Engineering must confirm the exact grade and project design values.

### 问：CFRP 碳纤维筋的目录参数是什么？

目录列出的 CFRP 碳纤维筋参数包括：直径 3-40 毫米、密度 1.5-1.6 g/cm3、抗拉强度 1800-2500 MPa、弹性模量 120-165 GPa、伸长率 1.3%-1.5%。准确等级和项目设计取值必须由工程师确认。

## GFRP rockbolts / GFRP 锚杆

### Q: What GFRP rockbolt types are available?

The catalogue presents all-thread or solid GFRP rockbolts and soil nails, hollow GFRP rockbolts for grouting, self-drilling GFRP rockbolts for soft soil or incompetent beds, and GFRP anchor or cable bolts for long-bolt and confined-space applications.

### 问：Oceanpower 提供哪些 GFRP 锚杆？

目录包括全螺纹或实心 GFRP 锚杆和土钉、中空注浆 GFRP 锚杆、适用于软土或难以预钻孔地层的自钻式 GFRP 锚杆，以及适用于长锚杆和受限空间的 GFRP 锚索。

## Form tie rods and mesh / 对拉杆与网格

### Q: What is the catalogue specification for the 17 mm GFRP form tie rod?

The catalogue example for a 17 mm GFRP form tie rod gives a tensile load of 150 kN, tensile strength of 800 MPa, elasticity modulus of at least 45 GPa, and shear strength of at least 120 MPa.

### 问：17 毫米 GFRP 对拉杆的目录参数是什么？

目录中的 17 毫米 GFRP 对拉杆示例参数为：拉伸载荷 150 kN、抗拉强度 800 MPa、弹性模量不低于 45 GPa、抗剪强度不低于 120 MPa。

### Q: What are the catalogue examples for GFRP mesh?

The catalogue examples include rod diameters of 4 mm and 6 mm, spacing of 100 x 100 mm, a standard panel size of 1200 x 1800 mm, rod tensile strength above 1000 N/mm2, and elasticity modulus above 40 GPa.

### 问：GFRP 网格的目录示例参数是什么？

目录示例包括 4 毫米和 6 毫米杆径、100 x 100 毫米间距、1200 x 1800 毫米标准板尺寸、杆材抗拉强度大于 1000 N/mm2，以及弹性模量大于 40 GPa。
