## FRP overview

FRP means fiber-reinforced polymer/plastic. The catalogue presents glass (GFRP), basalt (BFRP), carbon (CFRP), and aramid fiber systems. FRP combines reinforcing fiber with a resin matrix. Catalogue-listed resin families include unsaturated polyester, epoxy, vinyl ester, and polyurethane.

Catalogue positioning includes corrosion resistance, high tensile strength, low weight, non-conductivity/nonmagnetic behavior, and cuttability. Suitability depends on the exact product, resin, environment, design, and applicable standard.

Source: Catalogue, FRP Introduction, pages labelled 5/6 and 7/8.

## BFRP rebar

Catalogue positioning: basalt-fiber reinforcement, nonmagnetic, electrically insulating, acid/alkali resistant, temperature resistant, and heat/sound insulating.

Catalogue reference values include:

- Tensile strength: at least 750 MPa.
- Tensile modulus: at least 4.0 x 10^3 MPa as printed in the catalogue; engineering must verify this unit/value before public use.
- Elongation: at least 1.8%.
- Alkali resistance, strength retention: at least 85%.

Applications named include docks, dams, mining roadway support, reinforcement construction, concrete roads and bridges, corrosive environments, hospital NMR/CT facilities, optical cable reinforcement, telecom towers, and transformer substations.

Source: Catalogue, Basalt FRP Rebar, page labelled 13.

## CFRP rebar

Catalogue reference values:

- Diameter: 3-40 mm.
- Density: 1.5-1.6 g/cm3.
- Tensile strength: 1800-2500 MPa.
- Elasticity modulus: 120-165 GPa.
- Elongation: 1.3-1.5%.
- Coefficient of thermal expansion printed as 0 x 10^-6 / C.

The catalogue positions CFRP for excellent tensile strength and low weight. Engineering must confirm exact grade and project design values.

Source: Catalogue, Carbon FRP Rebar, page labelled 14.

## GFRP rockbolt systems

The catalogue presents these types:

- all-thread/solid GFRP rockbolt or soil nail;
- hollow GFRP rockbolt for grouting anchors;
- self-drilling GFRP rockbolt for incompetent beds or soft soil where pre-drilling is difficult;
- GFRP anchor/cable bolt for long bolts, confined spaces, face stabilization, and slope protection.

Catalogue-listed advantages include high tensile strength, cuttability, low weight, flexibility, non-conductivity, and corrosion resistance. Exact load, diameter, torque, thread, flame/antistatic requirement, coupler, nut, tray, and drill-bit selection require engineering confirmation.

Source: Catalogue, pages labelled 9/10, 15/16, and 19/20.

## Qualification questions

Ask one question at a time, choosing the most useful missing field:

1. What is the application: tunnel, bridge, coastal structure, mining, roadway, foundation, formwork, mesh, or another use?
2. Is the reinforcement temporary or permanent?
3. What environment or constraint matters most: corrosion, conductivity, magnetism, cuttability, weight, acid/alkali, or another condition?
4. Which product and diameter are specified or being considered?
5. What is the estimated quantity and unit?
6. What standard or consultant specification applies?
7. What is the delivery country/port and required date?
8. What company and business email should receive the human-reviewed response?
